Nutrition points today ? What does this number mean?

This is the change to your Rebuild points from today's food check-ins. It is not the amount of food you logged.

Rebuild scores the foods you enter and converts their average score into points. A neutral result, or a score with no matching point value, can show as 0.

So 0 does not mean that no food was entered. Check the entries below to see what was logged.

Your nutrition history will appear after your first check-ins.

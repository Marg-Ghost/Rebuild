How is this estimate calculated?

It averages the available sleep, food and activity scores. Sleep starts at 100 for 7–9 hours and drops for shorter or longer sleep; timing can subtract up to 15 points.

Stress adds 0.3 points per rating point, up to 3. The estimate then falls at a fixed 10 points per hour. The timer shows when it reaches 20%.

Workload is not part of this score. It helps shape activity suggestions. This formula is not a measurement of actual focus or productivity.
